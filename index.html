CLEAN WATER

Why Water Matters

Water is essential for every living organism. People need safe water for drinking, cooking, cleaning, agriculture and maintaining healthy communities.

Access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene helps prevent diseases and improves the quality of life.

OUR WATER SOURCES

Protect Our Rivers

Rivers, lakes, wetlands and groundwater are important sources of water for people, animals and ecosystems.

Protecting these sources from pollution helps preserve nature and ensures water availability for future generations.

Learn About Pollution

ENVIRONMENTAL THREAT

Water Pollution

Water pollution occurs when harmful substances enter rivers, lakes, groundwater or other water bodies.

Plastic waste, untreated wastewater, chemicals and other pollutants can damage aquatic ecosystems and threaten human health.
